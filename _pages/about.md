🧑‍💻 I hold a PhD in Intelligent Transport Systems, focussing on optimising transport systems using smart mobility data.

I am passionate about visualising, exploring and modelling data, particularly geo-spatial data.

My PhD examined novel methods for predicting UK bikeshare demand and optimising station network structure. 

It was the first to study multiple UK cities simultaneously, developing a hierarchical model that estimated regional and local effects of the urban environment. 

To address the complexity of optimising station network structure, I adapted an Evolutionary Algorithm (EA), integrating urban characteristics into its objective function. 

The approach ensured that station network design adapted to urban context, maximised predicted ridership and minimised overall journey impedance.

Interests
------
I am fascinated in understanding the cities we live in, seeking out new ways to obtain and analyse geo-spatial data. From measuring graph structures of road networks, quantifying building characteristics, or discovering new urban zones across via unsupervised learning techniques. By integrating multiple sources of data together, underlying  urban structures can be captured in new (sometimes exciting!) ways.

I primarily work with Python for geo-spatial data wrangling and machine learning, complemented by R for statistical modelling.
